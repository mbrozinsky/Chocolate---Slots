    How to play

    You start with 100 play credits. Each spin costs one credit and draws ten different cards from a fresh 78-card deck. The longest pattern sets your payout. A five-card pattern earns back the cost of the spin.

    Chocolate Chain. Both numbers rise from card to card. For example, (2, 4), (4, 7), (6, 9).

    No Chocolate group. In any pair, neither card has both numbers strictly higher than the other. For example, (1, 10), (2, 9), (2, 8).

    Cards can appear anywhere on the reels. Light blue highlights show one longest pattern. When both types tie, the game shows the chain.

    After a paying spin, choose Show winning cards to group the blue cards from left to right, starting in the top row. Choose Show original order to put the cards back where they landed.

    Sound. Reel clicks begin when you press Spin. A chime marks a paying hand, and a fanfare celebrates a jackpot or Super Bonus. Use Sound on or Sound off to switch the effects. Adjust the volume on your device.

    When a spin pays out, the hand score is the sum of both numbers on every highlighted card.

    Super Bonus. Each game draws a target from 70 through 75 and displays it above the reels. Earn 100 extra play credits when the highlighted paying pattern scores exactly that target and its red numbers, in increasing order, form an arithmetic progression with a positive common difference. For example, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 qualifies. All highlighted cards must belong to that progression. The bonus is available on every spin and is added to the regular payout. Choose New game to draw a target.
