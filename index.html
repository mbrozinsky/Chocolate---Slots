    How to play

    You start with 100 play credits. Each spin costs one credit and draws ten different cards from a fresh 78-card deck. The longest pattern sets your payout. A five-card pattern earns back the cost of the spin.

    Chocolate Chain. Both numbers rise from card to card. For example, (2, 4), (4, 7), (6, 9).

    No Chocolate group. In any pair, neither card has both numbers strictly higher than the other. For example, (1, 10), (2, 9), (2, 8).

    Cards can appear anywhere on the reels. Gold highlights show one longest pattern. When both types tie, the game shows the chain.
